## II. References and Statistical Limits

The executable and theoretical portfolios use the full CSI 300 definition, round-lot and cost rules appropriate to each benchmark, and normalized NAV. Their differences from the market index reflect portfolio construction and exposure as well as execution constraints. Return alone cannot isolate selection skill.

No August 28 matched-null court was evaluated for this newly reconstructed season. The 11,000-control evaluations at September 30 and October 9 belong to those later cumulative cutoffs; their p-values are not evidence for this July–August window.

**Evidence boundary**: NAV, returns, rankings and drawdown are Level A observations. All contestants were selected before this reconstruction, but the reconstruction itself is retrospective and does not establish a verifiable pre-market commitment. Sector, style and durable forecasting explanations remain unidentified. Figures use unrounded public NAV; displayed NAV has four decimals.
