# QuantPits Graveyard Arena — Capital Granularity Diagnostics

Run ID: `full_tournament_extended_zoo`

## 1. Unified Execution Environment Constraints

All contestants and animal portfolios in this Arena were executed under strictly standardized real-world capital constraints:

- **Initial Capital**: `CNY 500,000` (fixed benchmark per portfolio)
- **Lot Sizing Rule**: `lot_size = 100` (China A-share standard; fractional / partial-lot buying is disabled)
- **Affordability Rule**: If allocated cash for an instrument cannot purchase 1 lot (`price * 100 > allocated_cash`), the BUY order is **skipped** (unaffordable skip)
- **Residual Cash Rule**: Residual unspent cash remains in the cash account without secondary redistribution or round-up leverage
- **Universe Exit Rule**: Out-of-pool holdings are prioritized for exit, strictly bounded by the animal's `DropN` quota

## 2. Research Interpretation & Methodology

For broader portfolios with large `TopK` (e.g., Eagle-44/66/88, Whale Shark, Taotie), performance variations must **not** be automatically interpreted as pure signal breadth sensitivity.

Under fixed CNY 500,000 capital:
1. As target capacity grows, theoretical cash allocation per stock decreases (`500k / K`);
2. Higher-priced stocks naturally trigger the 1-lot affordability constraint and get skipped;
3. Actual holdings may systematically diverge from theoretical target capacity, leading to natural cash retention;
4. Therefore, these animals stress-test the compound interaction of:  
   $$\text{Effective Performance} = \text{Signal Breadth} \times \text{Portfolio Policy} \times \text{Finite-Capital Granularity}$$
This is **not** a simulation artifact or defect, but a deliberately preserved realistic execution boundary.

## 3. Taotie Benchmark Definition

> **Taotie (饕餮)** is explicitly defined as a **capital-constrained full-universe executable benchmark**, rather than a theoretical unconstrained equal-weight index. Under CNY 500,000 capital, it represents the realistic portfolio formed when attempting to track the eligible universe purely through passive exit/entry rebalancing without fractional shares.
